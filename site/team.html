The Founder's Journey

"I grew up fascinated by how software could command mechanical joints and bring physical machines to life. Starting with autonomous rovers and robotic limbs in our university laboratory, FUTURE ROBOTIX was established to build practical, deployable commercial robots right here in India — combining robust engineering, transparent pricing, and homegrown manufacturing."
