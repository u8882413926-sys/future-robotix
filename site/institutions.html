Student outcomes

What students take away.

Our campus build programs move students from passive consumers of technology to confident robotics creators. Students work hands-on across three core domains:

1. Mechanical Assembly

Chassis rigging, servo alignment, gear reductions, and kinematic balance.

2. Avionics & Circuits

LiFePO4 battery management, motor drivers, sensor busses, and cable harness design.

3. Autonomous Code

Python control loops, sensor fusion algorithms, and obstacle avoidance scripting.
