Curriculum & Practical Competencies

Measurable Student Learning Outcomes.

Our curriculum transitions students from theoretical textbook engineering to confident physical systems creators across three technical pillars:

1. Precision Mechatronics

Chassis assembly, high-torque BLDC motor integration, planetary gear reduction, and structural center-of-gravity tuning.

2. Avionics & Power Architecture

CAN bus communications, LiFePO4 battery management (BMS), motor driver tuning, and industrial wire harness design.

3. Autonomous Algorithms & ROS 2

LiDAR SLAM mapping, sensor fusion (IMU + depth cameras), real-time path planning, and autonomous obstacle avoidance.
