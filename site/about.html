Our story.

FUTURE ROBOTIX starts from a simple idea: the robots we imagined as children should exist, and they should be within reach of the people who want them.

Founded by robotics engineer Uday Sahu in New Delhi, FUTURE ROBOTIX was established to turn advanced robotics research into reliable, accessible commercial platforms. What began as prototype development in university robotics clubs has grown into an Udyam-registered micro-enterprise developing modular humanoids, autonomous service companions, and hands-on educational platforms for Indian organizations.

Our mission.

Robots in every corner of the world. We want to recreate the future we imagined as children, one robot at a time.

Safety and standards.

Every FUTURE ROBOTIX unit incorporates emergency mechanical E-stops, optical sensor obstacle buffers, current-limiting motor controllers, and smart battery management systems (BMS). All robots undergo 48 hours of autonomous burn-in navigation and safety validation in our New Delhi workshop prior to client delivery.
