Commercial Terms

Order & Procurement Terms

Last updated: 1 November 2026

1. Order Requests Are Non-Binding Inquiries

Submitting an order or demonstration request through this Website does not constitute a binding purchase contract. No financial charge is processed at the submission stage. A legally binding agreement is formed exclusively when: (a) FUTURE ROBOTIX issues an official written itemized quotation following an on-site feasibility review, and (b) both parties execute a formal purchase order or agreement.

2. Pricing Estimates & Quotations

  • Website figures represent baseline ex-factory estimates in Indian Rupees (INR).
  • Final costs reflect custom hardware adaptations, specific sensor modules, operational software licenses, and on-site deployment requirements confirmed during site surveys.
  • Applicable GST (18%) and transit freight are itemized transparently in all written quotations.

3. Fulfillment Workflow

  1. Inquiry Review: Our engineering team reviews facility specifications within 1 to 2 business days.
  2. On-Site Evaluation & Demo: Initial feasibility visits and robot demonstrations are coordinated in Delhi NCR and major educational clusters.
  3. Formal Proposal: We provide a comprehensive quotation outlining delivery schedules, training, and acceptance testing.
  4. Execution: Work commences upon mutual sign-off of the quotation and agreed advance disbursement.

4. Institutional Campus Build Programs

For educational institutions choosing the campus build pathway:

  • FUTURE ROBOTIX supplies all mechanical components, actuators, embedded circuit boards, firmware, and mentor guidance.
  • The institution provides a suitable lab space, standard 230V electrical connections, and student cohort supervision.
  • The completed physical robot remains the permanent property of the institution for lab study and display.

5. Warranty & Technical Support

All finished commercial units include a 12-Month Limited Hardware Warranty covering motor drivers, central compute units, and structural integrity under normal operating conditions. Technical support is accessible Monday through Saturday via support@futurerobotix.in.

6. Cancellation & Modifications

Order requests may be freely cancelled without obligation at any point prior to formal written agreement execution. Cancellation terms following formal sign-off are governed by the specific project milestone agreement.

7. Dispute Resolution

In the event of any disagreement, the parties agree to seek resolution through amicable dialogue within 30 days. Unresolved disputes shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the competent courts of New Delhi, India.
