Your personal data is processed strictly for the following lawful purposes:

4. Data Retention Periods

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the stated purposes:

  • Commercial Orders & Accounting Records: Retained for 8 years to fulfill statutory auditing and taxation obligations under Indian law.

5. Student & Campus Workshop Data

During campus build workshops at schools and universities, all student participation rosters are administered exclusively by the host academic institution. FUTURE ROBOTIX does not collect, harvest, or store personal or biometric records of students participating in campus sessions.

6. Your Rights Under DPDP Act 2023

Under Indian data protection legislation, you possess the right to:

  • Access a summary of your personal data processed by us.
  • Request the correction, completion, or updating of inaccurate records.
  • Request the erasure of personal data that is no longer required for statutory obligations.
  • Register a grievance with our designated Grievance Officer.
