Dimensions & Form Factor

22 cm Diameter · Seamless spherical polycarbonate shell

Net Weight

1.6 kg

Drive Kinematics

Dual-motor internal differential pendulum drive with gyroscopic momentum wheel for 360° omnidirectional rolling

Maximum Velocity

Up to 2.2 m/s (approx. 8 km/h); rolls smoothly over carpet, hardwood, tiles, asphalt, and low grass

Shell Material & Ingress Protection

High-impact polycarbonate seamless shell; IP65 waterproof and dustproof; drop-resistant up to 1.2 m onto concrete

Battery Architecture & Charging

Internal 7.4V 3200 mAh Li-ion battery (4 Hours active dynamic rolling); Qi wireless inductive charging dock

Sensor Suite

High-bandwidth 9-DOF IMU (3-axis gyro, 3-axis accelerometer, 3-axis magnetometer), optical surface velocity encoder, ambient light sensor

Programmable Illumination

Internal 32-LED RGB addressable halo ring with programmable lighting choreography, telemetry indicators, and mood states

Wireless Telemetry

Bluetooth 5.0 Low Energy (up to 30 m line-of-sight range) with optional 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi educational lab bridge

Programming Software Ecosystem

Browser-based Scratch block-coding IDE, Python 3 SDK with real-time telemetry streaming, and native ROS 2 node

Educational Curriculum

40-Project lab experiment handbook, student challenge arena mats, and instructor lesson plans aligned with CBSE/AICTE standards

Safety & Construction

Zero exposed moving parts or pinch hazards; hermetically sealed against liquids and dust
