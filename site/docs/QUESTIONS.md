# A. Questions by priority

## P1: Must answer before launch

### Registration and legal identity
1. What is your Udyam registration number (format `UDYAM-XX-00-0000000`)?
2. What is FUTURE's legal structure: proprietorship, partnership, LLP or something else? You said it is not a Private Limited company; please confirm. (Udyam registration works for any of these structures, so I will describe it only as MSME registration.)
3. Do you have a GSTIN? If not, I will remove it everywhere.
4. What is the registered business address?
5. Can we publish a copy of your Udyam certificate? If yes, please upload it with personal details such as phone number, email and any ID numbers hidden.

### Robots (needed for `robots.json`)
For each robot:
6. Name, slug (short web address word), one-line purpose, tagline.
7. A 2 to 4 sentence description in plain words.
8. Size (dimensions and weight) and key specifications.
9. The list of operations it can perform, with one sentence each.
10. Price to buy (base price), and the price added by each operation. Say whether each add-on is one-time or recurring.
11. Build-on-campus prices: kit price, mentor day rate, material cost per student, and minimum number of students.
12. Real photos or renders, with a file name for each.

### Contact and operations
13. Official email addresses: hello@, orders@, support@. Which one receives order requests?
14. Phone number(s), postal address and working hours.
15. How quickly will you promise to reply (for example within 2 working days)?
16. How do you want to be notified when an order arrives: email, WhatsApp, Telegram or something else?
17. What is your domain name?

### Legal drafts (give these to your lawyer)
18. Who is the Grievance Officer for privacy complaints (name and email)? It can be you.
19. Which city's courts should apply, and do you prefer courts or arbitration for disputes?
20. Payment terms: advance amount, balance due date, accepted payment methods.
21. Whether displayed prices include GST, and the rate that applies.
22. Warranty period and what it covers.
23. Cancellation and refund terms after a customer accepts your written quotation.
24. Is the site visit free or charged? What happens if the customer declines after the visit?
25. Who owns the finished robot and its software, especially when students build it?
26. Who is responsible for student safety and supervision during a campus build?
27. Which providers will you use for hosting, email and storing forms? (Needed in the Privacy Policy.)
28. Will the site use analytics or any cookies besides the essential ones?
29. How long do you want to keep contact messages and order records? (Your accountant can advise on accounting records.)
30. During campus builds, will you collect any data about students? If so, how will you get parent or guardian consent for students under 18?

### Trust
